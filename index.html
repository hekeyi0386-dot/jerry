About

I'm a quantitative researcher with a background in economics and financial mathematics, currently pursuing my M.S. at the University of Chicago. My work sits at the intersection of high-frequency trading, market microstructure, and machine learning.

Across three internships in China's quantitative finance industry, I've built HFT factor pipelines, developed order-flow models using Hawkes processes, and collaborated with traders on real-time risk monitoring systems.

I'm particularly interested in limit order book dynamics, regime-switching models, and the application of modern ML methods to financial time series.
